Layout

Hero, Block, Layer, Grid, and layout utility classes.

Hero

A large title area. As the first child of <main>, it extends over the padding of <main> and fills its width, which excludes the sidebar. Outside <main>, it fills the width of the viewport. The top of this page is a Hero.

Set a background image with --hero-image. A relative URL in a custom property may not resolve against the page, so use a path that starts with / or a full URL.

<div class="hero" style="--hero-image: url('/images/header/wide.webp')">
    <h1>Title</h1>
    <p>Subtitle</p>
</div>

Block

An area with a border. Children are stacked vertically with a gap of 16px. When the first child is <b>, it is colored as the title of the variant.

Responsive

Classes in responsive-design.css show or hide elements depending on the viewport width. hide and show always apply. small: applies up to 768px, medium: from 769px to 1080px, and large: from 1081px. Resize the window to see them change.

Small (up to 768px): hide small:show

Medium (769px to 1080px): hide medium:show

Large (from 1081px): hide large:show

Hidden up to 768px: small:hide

<nav class="small:hide">…</nav>
<button class="hide small:show">…</button>
Warning

The show classes set display: block. For elements that need another display value, such as flex or grid, do not use hide; combine the *:hide classes instead.

Overflow

overflow-show, overflow-hide, and overflow-scroll change how content that does not fit is handled.

overflow-show: content that does not fit is still drawn outside the box.

overflow-hide: content that does not fit is clipped. Content that does not fit is clipped.

overflow-scroll: content that does not fit can be scrolled. Content that does not fit can be scrolled.

unselectable prevents text from being selected. selectable makes text selectable again inside an unselectable element.

Priority of utility classes

Classes that change a single property (the classes on this page, fg-color-*, font-weight-*, and so on) are declared with !important. They take precedence over component styles and style attributes, regardless of load order or specificity.
