Tables

Tables and their variants, defined in table.css.

Basic

Applies to <table> without a class. Other elements can get the same look with the table class. Header cells in <thead> have a background.

Component Stylesheet Script

<table>
    <thead>
        <tr><th>Name</th><th>Value</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
        <tr><td>One</td><td>1</td></tr>
    </tbody>
</table>

Variants

Variants can be combined.

ClassEffect
table-stripedAdds a background to even rows in <tbody>.
table-hoverAdds a background to the row under the pointer.
table-borderedAdds borders to every cell.
