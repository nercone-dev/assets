Progress

Linear, Circular, and Logo progress indicators.

Shows how much of a task is done. progress.js reads the value from aria-valuenow, within aria-valuemin (default 0) and aria-valuemax (default 100). Without aria-valuenow, the progress is indeterminate.

Linear

Circular and logo

Range

aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="5" aria-valuenow="3" is 60%.

Customization

Change the size with --progress-size (default 48px) and --progress-thickness (default 4px), the indeterminate animation with --progress-duration, and the colors with --color-progress and --color-progress-track.

Usage

<div class="progress-linear" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload" aria-valuenow="40"></div>

<svg class="progress-circular" viewBox="0 0 48 48" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload" aria-valuenow="40">
    <circle class="progress-track"     cx="24" cy="24" r="20" pathLength="1"/>
    <circle class="progress-indicator" cx="24" cy="24" r="20" pathLength="1"/>
</svg>
