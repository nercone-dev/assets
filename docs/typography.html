Details

<details> is drawn as a summary bar and a content area. The triangle at the end of the summary points up while it is open.

Closed

Content.

Open

Children are spaced by --details-padding (default 16px) on the sides and top. The summary padding is --details-summary-padding (default 12px 16px).

    <p>Text</p>
